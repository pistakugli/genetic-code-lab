# Agent Note: Family-level break atlas for ordered-path OOD artifacts

Status: implemented

## Context

The why-report layer explains one recorded break at a time, and it only fired
on rescue measurements, which existed in a single one-fact artifact. The
five-case OOD suite recorded seventeen broken paraphrases with only eight
rescue searches and no replacement searches, so most recorded breaks carried
no classification at all, and no artifact stated what the recorded population
of breaks looked like as a whole. The research note's hand-written family
table could not be regenerated by the repo's own tooling.

## Decision

Extended `tools/llm_reader/why_report.py` and added
`tools/llm_reader/why_atlas.py`:

- every broken paraphrase now gets a break classification
  (`paraphraseBreaks` per case), not only the rescue-searched subset, with an
  honest `rescueSought` flag and the layer-shift outcome when a search
  exists; `attach_why_analysis` fires for any case with rescue measurements
  or at least one broken paraphrase;
- rescue entries gained an `attempted` flag so a historical run without a
  replacement search reads "not run" instead of the misleading "did not
  find"; historical rescue items without a family are joined back to the
  case's paraphrase list by exact prompt match;
- the atlas module aggregates every recorded paraphrase replay across
  labeled artifacts into per-family and per-source break taxonomies with
  rescue coverage, and `verify_break_atlas` recomputes every count, tally,
  and mean from the raw paraphrase/rescue fields so a fabricated atlas fails;
- the lane verifier (`verify_ordered_path_ood_stress.py`) now recomputes the
  paraphrase-break classifications, rescue-sought flags, attempted flags, and
  the new aggregates; artifacts recorded before this layer keep verifying
  under the previous rules.

## Alternatives considered

- **Aggregate only the rescue-searched breaks:** rejected because that
  subset is not a random sample of breaks; the atlas reports coverage
  (10/19 searched) instead of silently generalizing it.
- **Derive the atlas from the why blocks:** rejected because the verifier
  must be independent of the layer it verifies; the atlas reads only raw
  paraphrase/rescue fields.
- **Hand-write the family table in the research note again:** rejected
  because prose numbers that no gate recomputes drift from the measurements.

## Consequences

New-format artifacts must classify every broken paraphrase or verification
fails. The markdown report gains a per-subject paraphrase-breaks table. The
atlas produced a population statement the repo did not have: across the four
recorded GPT-2 artifacts, 19 of 72 measured replays broke, 18 as sign-flips
and 1 as a relative falloff, the imperative family broke 7/7, the matched
standard baseline broke 0/30, and of the 10 rescue-searched breaks 6
recovered by shifting the same components to a neighbouring layer — which
shows the France replacement-transfer case that motivated the why layer is
atypical, not representative. The atlas states rescue coverage explicitly and
claims nothing about the unsearched remainder beyond break classification.

## Verification

`scripts/build-why-atlas.py` (worktree) enriches the four recorded artifacts
in memory (historical files are not mutated) and runs: positive atlas
recomputation, the full lane verifier over the enriched five-case OOD
artifact (17 broken paraphrases, 8 rescue searches), and four tamper negative
controls (family break count, break-kind tally, rescue coverage, mean
ratio). All pass. `scripts/verify-why-report-cycle.py` grew five further
negative controls (fabricated paraphrase break kind, stripped paraphrase
breaks, fabricated rescue-sought flag, fabricated attempted flag, fabricated
break aggregate); all eleven checks pass, including backward compatibility
with the historical artifact. The runner markdown integration check now
covers the paraphrase-breaks table anchors. Review copies live under
`download/adaptive-circuit-reader-demo/` (`gpt2-break-atlas.json`,
`gpt2-break-atlas.md`, regenerated why-report JSON/markdown, full runner
markdown).
